# SM-137: The delayed job heap is ordered by the delay, not by the due time

- Original: `STL_push_heap_DelayJob` (0x681190 / 0x681280) of `CServerEventMgr::ProcessDelayJobs` (0x67da50), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `ServerEventDelayJobGreater` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/ServerEventMgr.h`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

The heap of CServerEventMgr compares the delay spans (CServerEventDelayJob +0x0C) of the jobs;
the start time (+0x10) is ignored. ProcessDelayJobs stops at the first job on top that is not
due.

## Effect

A job appended later with a short delay that is not due yet sits on top of an older job whose
longer delay has passed already, and holds it (and every job below) back until it is due
itself. Begins and ends of events, rewards and rollbacks can therefore run late. The rewrite
orders the heap the same way.

## Fix

Order the heap by start + delay.
