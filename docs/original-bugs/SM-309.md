# SM-309: Record lists freed through the wrong table

- Original: `AQ_DB_LoadCharSkills` (0x485a10), `AQ_DB_LoadCharTrainingCamp` (0x485b50),
  SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `AQ_DB_LoadCharSkills` and `AQ_DB_LoadCharTrainingCamp` in
  `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/AQDBLoaders.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

When the second select of `AQ_DB_LoadCharSkills` (`_SkillMastery`) fails, the partly filled
mastery list is freed through the `_Skill` table. When the buff select of
`AQ_DB_LoadCharTrainingCamp` fails, the buff list is freed through the camp member table.

## Effect

`CDBTable::FreeRecord` hands the records to the allocator of the other table. With the default
allocator the records are deleted through their virtual destructor and nothing is visible; an
allocator that pools records per class would receive records of a foreign class. The rewrite
does the same.

## Fix

Free each list through the table that created its records.
