# SM-134: The Anubis and Isis temple opening is logged as "Anubis And Neith"

- Original: `CShardSJ_TempleOfAnubisAndIsisGate::OnTick` (0x660d50), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CShardSJ_TempleOfAnubisAndIsisGate::s_pszOpenLog` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/ShardTempleGate.cpp`
- Severity: cosmetic

## What the original does

When the Anubis and Isis temples open, the job logs
"Scheduleing : Anubis And Neith Of Temple is now Opened ...".

## Effect

The log names the Neith temple, which belongs to the Selkis and Neith gate. The rewrite logs
the same text.

## Fix

Log "Anubis And Isis Of Temple".
