# SM-145: The running copy of a schedule job is never freed

- Original: `CShard_ScheduleExecutor::EndJob` (0x667690, slot 3 of the shard executor), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CShard_ScheduleExecutor::EndScheduleJob` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/ShardSchedule.cpp`
- Severity: minor (memory)

## What the original does

BeginJob (0x6675c0) asks the registered prototype for a copy: the job's CreateInstance makes a
new object and keeps it in the prototype's m_pInstance; the copy's own m_pInstance stays NULL.
At the end of the period EndJob calls DeleteInstance (0x664980) on the running copy, which
deletes the copy's m_pInstance - NULL - and returns.

## Effect

Every begun schedule job leaks its copy (0x78 to 0xA0 bytes, plus the open-time list of the
temple gate jobs) when its period ends. The prototype's m_pInstance points to the last copy
until the next begin overwrites it. BeginJob also keeps the copy when AddJob fails (only a log
line).

## Fix

Call DeleteInstance on the prototype (FindRegisteredJob(info.strDefine)) or delete the copy
directly, after giving IScheduleJob a virtual destructor (SM-132).
