# SM-351: The guild and training camp queries leak their records

- Original: `AQ_CharDataSelector::QueryGuild` (0x46b830) and
  `AQ_CharDataSelector::QueryTrainingCamp` (0x46ba20), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `AQ_CharDataSelector::QueryGuild` / `QueryTrainingCamp` in
  `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/AQCharDataQueries.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

`AQ_DB_LoadGuildData` selects the guild, its members, its alliance and its siege fortress;
`AQ_DB_LoadCharTrainingCamp` selects the camp, its members and their buffs. After the answer is
written, the queries destroy all these lists as plain `std::list`s. The records are only handed
back to their tables by the loaders when a select fails (and not at all when the loader fails
after a successful select, e.g. two guilds of the same name).

## Effect

Every guild or training camp query of the console leaks the selected record objects. The
rewrite does the same.

## Fix

Free every list through its table (`CDBObjectList_DetachAndClear`) at the end of both queries.
