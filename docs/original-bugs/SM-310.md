# SM-310: The character information query leaks its records

- Original: `AQ_CharDataSelector::QueryCharInfo` (0x469c50), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `AQ_CharDataSelector::QueryCharInfo` in
  `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/AQCharDataQueries.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

The `_Char` and `_CharTrijob` records selected for the information page are only returned to
their tables when the select fails. On success the record lists are destroyed as plain lists.

## Effect

Every successful information query of the console leaks two record objects. The rewrite does
the same.

## Fix

Free both lists through their tables (`CDBObjectList_DetachAndClear`) before returning.
