# SM-303: Reviving a character can drop another character from the delete schedule

- Original: `CShardMaintainer::RemoveFromDeleteBuckets` (0x431e10), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CShardMaintainer::RemoveFromDeleteBuckets` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/ShardMaintainer.cpp`
- Severity: major

## What the original does

The three bucket maps (keyed by CharID) are searched with `lower_bound` and the result is only
compared with `end()`. The first bucket that has any CharID at or above the revived one is
taken, and that entry is released and erased.

## Effect

When the revived character is not in the first bucket searched (or in none), another
character with a higher CharID loses its entry: it is not deleted permanently on time (until
the next start). The rewrite does the same.

## Fix

Use `find`, or compare the key of the `lower_bound` result.
