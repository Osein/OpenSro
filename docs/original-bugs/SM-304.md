# SM-304: The job timeout check steps from an erased map node

- Original: `COneByOneProcessor::CheckJobTimeouts` (0x41cf90), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `COneByOneProcessor::CheckJobTimeouts` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/OneByOneProcessor.cpp`
- Severity: major

## What the original does

The loop walks the job map and calls `CompleteJob` for every job older than 60 s.
`CompleteJob` erases the job's map node; the loop then increments the iterator of that erased
(freed) node.

## Effect

Once a silk history job times out, the next step reads freed memory: the walk may skip jobs,
loop on garbage or fault the processor thread. The rewrite does the same.

## Fix

Take the next iterator before completing the job.
